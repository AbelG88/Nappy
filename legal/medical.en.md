# Medical notice

*Version 1.0 · November 4, 2026*

- Nappy is a **notebook to organize your observations**. It is not a medical product or service.
- It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease, and it does not replace a consultation with a health professional.
- Statistics, charts and reports show only what you recorded. They do not indicate whether a child's development is normal or "behind".
- There are no expected ages or mandatory goals: every child and every family has their own pace.
- If you have questions about health, development, behavior, or signs such as pain when peeing or pooping, blood, fever, persistent constipation or marked regressions, talk to their pediatrician.
- In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
- If you share the report with a professional, it is a complement to the consultation, not a substitute.
