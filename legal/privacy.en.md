# Privacy Policy

*Version 1.0 · November 4, 2026*

Nappy is designed so your data stays on your device. Here is a plain account of what is stored, where, and what does (and does not) leave your device.

## 1. Controller

**Abel García**, as an individual, Argentina. Contact: Settings → Contact or github.com/AbelG88/Nappy.

## 2. What data the app handles

What you enter yourself:

- **Child profile:** name, date of birth and stage of the process.
- **Caregivers:** names or roles (for example, "Mom", "Grandma").
- **Records:** moments and results (pee, poop, accidents, toilet use), time, notes and similar data.
- **Settings:** reminders, backup preferences, an email address for backups (if you enter one) and internal counters (for example, to decide when to show a support request).

This data can reveal information about a **child's health and development**, which Argentine law treats as sensitive. That is why the app encrypts it and does not send it to any server of mine.

## 3. Where it is stored

- **Only on your device**, in the browser's local storage, **encrypted** (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your PIN or password using PBKDF2). The key only lives in memory while the app is unlocked.
- **I do not receive, see or have any way to recover** this data or your PIN.
- The app locks itself after a period of inactivity (configurable).

## 4. What leaves your device

**None of your records leave on their own.** Only what you decide to send does:

- **Encrypted backup (.nappy):** you save it or share it with your device's share menu. It is protected by the password you choose.
- **Shared history:** an **unencrypted** file with the child's data. Who you send it to is your decision.
- **PDF report:** generated on your device.
- **Email:** if you use email delivery, the app opens your email app with a prepared message. You send it, and your email provider processes it. **Contact** messages work the same way: I only receive them if you send them.
- **Support links:** tapping them takes you to Mercado Pago, Cafecito, PayPal or Buy Me a Coffee, which handle your data under their own policies.

## 5. Third-party services that see your connection

To load the app, your browser requests resources from external servers. Those services receive technical connection data, such as your **IP address**, browser and date and time, **but not your records**:

- **GitHub Pages** (hosts the app).
- **Tailwind CDN** (cdn.tailwindcss.com, styles).
- **Google Fonts** (typefaces).

Each has its own privacy policy. I do not receive that information, and the app includes no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It uses the browser's local storage, which is necessary for it to work.

## 6. Notifications

If you turn them on, reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. I do not use any external notification service.

## 7. Your rights

The data is in your hands, so you control it directly:

- **Access and copy:** Settings → Data and backup.
- **Correction:** edit the records and profile in the app.
- **Deletion:** delete records or all data from Settings, or clear the site data in your browser.

Since I do not hold your data, I cannot access, correct or delete it for you. In Argentina, the supervisory authority under Law 25,326 on Personal Data Protection is the Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), where you may file complaints. If you are in another country, you may have additional rights under your local law.

## 8. Children and teenagers

Nappy is for adults. The child's data is entered by their mother, father, guardian or authorized person. A minor must not use it.

## 9. Security

Encryption protects your data if someone accesses your device without your PIN, but no measure is infallible. Choose a PIN that is not obvious, keep your device and browser up to date and take care of the files you export. A device with malicious software, or someone who knows your PIN, can expose the data.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, I will publish a new dated version and ask you to accept it again.

## 11. Consent

By accepting the Terms and this Policy, you consent to the processing described, including of the sensitive data about the child that you enter yourself, and you confirm you have the right to do so. You can withdraw it at any time by ceasing to use the app and deleting the data.
